## 1. What JarCube is

**What is JarCube?**
JarCube is a customer conversation platform. It connects your WhatsApp Business
number, website chat widget, and social channels into one dashboard. Your team
replies from a shared inbox, an AI assistant answers routine questions on its
own, automated flows carry customers through booking or ordering, and broadcast
campaigns reach the segments you choose.

**What problem does JarCube solve?**
Most businesses lose customers to slow replies. Enquiries arrive on WhatsApp,
Instagram, and the website, and they sit unanswered until somebody is free.
JarCube answers instantly, collects the details you need, routes each chat to the
right person, and follows up automatically so nothing goes cold.

**Is JarCube a chatbot or a support desk?**
Both. JarCube includes an AI chatbot that answers from your own content, and a
shared team inbox where human agents take over when needed. The bot handles the
repetitive questions and hands off to a person when a conversation needs one.

**Who is JarCube for?**
JarCube suits any business that gets customer enquiries over chat. It is used
across healthcare, real estate, finance, distribution, education, home services,
e-commerce, and automobile dealerships. It works for a two-person team and for a
company running several departments.

**Do I need technical knowledge to use JarCube?**
No. The flow builder works by dragging blocks onto a canvas. Campaigns, the
knowledge base, and bot settings are all managed from the dashboard. No code is
required. If you do want to connect JarCube to your own systems, an API and
webhooks are available for your developers.

**How is JarCube different from just using WhatsApp on a phone?**
A phone allows one person to hold the number. JarCube puts your whole team behind
the same number with routing, assignment, internal notes, full chat history, an
AI assistant, automation, campaigns, and reporting. Nothing is trapped on one
device and nothing is lost when a staff member leaves.

## 5. WhatsApp message charges from Meta

**Does my JarCube subscription cover WhatsApp message fees?**
No. Meta charges per message at its own published rates, and those charges are
billed to you separately. Your JarCube subscription covers the platform itself:
the inbox, automation, AI, CRM, campaigns, and team features.

**Does JarCube add a markup on WhatsApp message charges?**
No. JarCube does not add any markup on top of what Meta charges. You pay Meta's
rates directly.

**Why are WhatsApp charges separate from my JarCube plan?**
WhatsApp message pricing is set by Meta and changes by country and message
category. Keeping it separate means you pay Meta's actual rate rather than a
padded estimate bundled into a subscription.

**Are website chat widget messages charged per message?**
No. The website chat widget is JarCube's own channel, so there are no Meta
per-message fees for it. Only WhatsApp messages carry Meta's charges.

## 6. WhatsApp setup and numbers

**Can I keep the WhatsApp number I already use?**
Usually yes. An existing number can be migrated to the WhatsApp Business API, or
we can set you up with a new one. There are some constraints depending on how the
number is currently registered, and we walk through them with you during
onboarding.

**Will I lose my old WhatsApp chats when migrating to JarCube?**
Migrating a number to the WhatsApp Business API means the number leaves the
regular WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business app. Existing chat history stays on the old
app and does not transfer. New conversations from then on live in JarCube.

**Can I use the same number on my phone and in JarCube?**
No. A number connected to the WhatsApp Business API is managed through the
platform rather than the phone app. That is what allows your whole team to work
the number at once from the shared inbox.

**How many WhatsApp numbers can I connect to JarCube?**
Starter supports 1 WhatsApp number, Growth supports 2, Business supports 5, and
Enterprise supports a custom number. Each number can have its own bot, flows, and
routing.

**How long does WhatsApp setup take with JarCube?**
Connecting a number is usually quick once Meta's business verification is in
place. Verification timing depends on Meta and how complete your business
documents are. We guide you through the steps during onboarding.

**Does JarCube use the official WhatsApp API?**
Yes. JarCube supports the official WhatsApp Cloud API as the default provider. An
alternative WhatsApp connection method is also available for specific cases, and
we advise which fits your situation.

**What are WhatsApp message templates?**
WhatsApp requires pre-approved templates to start conversations outside the
24-hour reply window. JarCube lets you compose these templates with variables
that fill in each contact's details, and use them in broadcast campaigns.

## 8. Shared inbox and team features

**What is the JarCube shared inbox?**
The JarCube shared inbox puts your whole team behind one business number or
channel. Chats arrive in a single queue, agents claim or are assigned
conversations, and everyone sees the full history. No conversation is stuck on one
person's phone.

**Can I route chats to different departments in JarCube?**
Yes. Group agents into departments such as sales, support, or accounts. Incoming
chats are routed to the department that should own them, so nobody triages a queue
that is not theirs.

**Can I control what each team member sees in JarCube?**
Yes. Roles and permissions decide what each person can see and do. Agents see the
conversations assigned to them, team leads see the whole queue, and sensitive
customer data stays visible only to the people who need it. Role-based access
control is available on Business and above.

**Can a chat be transferred to another agent in JarCube?**
Yes. Chats can be claimed, reassigned, or passed to another department
mid-conversation with the full history intact. The next agent sees everything that
was already said.

**Can the JarCube bot hand a chat to a human?**
Yes. The Transfer to Agent step hands the conversation to a human agent. When the
AI assistant reaches something it should not answer, it hands over rather than
guessing.

**Can my team leave private notes on a conversation?**
Yes. Internal notes let an agent leave context for whoever picks the chat up next.
Notes stay internal and never reach the customer.

**Does JarCube have quick replies?**
Yes. Quick replies let agents send saved answers to common questions in one click
instead of retyping them. Quick replies are available from the Starter plan.

**Can I add or remove team members in JarCube?**
Yes. You manage agents from the dashboard, adding or removing them and setting
their role and department. Your plan sets how many agent seats you have.

**Can two agents reply to the same customer at once in JarCube?**
Assignment prevents that. A conversation is owned by one agent at a time, so two
people do not send conflicting replies to the same customer.

**Does JarCube spread chats evenly across my agents?**
Yes. Incoming conversations are distributed across the available agents rather
than piling up on whoever replies fastest, so the workload stays balanced.

**Can I bring a colleague into a JarCube conversation without the customer
seeing?**
Yes. A colleague can be pulled into a conversation privately to advise or take
over. The internal discussion is never visible to the customer.

**Does JarCube support live chat in real time?**
Yes. Agents handle conversations live from a shared queue, so a high-priority
customer gets a real-time reply instead of waiting on a queued response.

## 9. AI assistant and knowledge base

**How does the JarCube AI assistant answer questions?**
The JarCube AI assistant is trained on your own content. It searches your
documents, website pages, and knowledge entries for the relevant passage, then
writes an answer grounded in what it found. It is not answering from generic
internet knowledge.

**Can I train the JarCube AI on my own documents?**
Yes. Upload your PDFs, price lists, policy documents, brochures, and internal
guides. The AI extracts the text, indexes it, and uses it when answering, so
customers get your details rather than an approximation.

**Which file types can I upload to train the JarCube AI?**
PDF, DOCX, and TXT files are supported. Each upload is parsed, split into
passages, and indexed. The dashboard shows the processing status and how many
passages were indexed from each file.

**Can the JarCube AI learn from my website?**
Yes. Point JarCube at your website and it reads the pages you nominate. Anything
already published on your site becomes something the assistant can answer from,
without you rewriting it.

**Can I add knowledge to the JarCube AI by typing it in?**
Yes. Besides uploading files, you can add knowledge entries directly as text with
a title. This suits short policies, opening hours, or answers that do not exist in
a document yet.

**Can I update or delete what the JarCube AI has learned?**
Yes. Knowledge entries and uploaded files can be viewed, replaced, or removed from
the dashboard. Removing an entry also removes it from the AI's search index, so it
stops being used in answers immediately.

**What happens if the JarCube AI does not know an answer?**
If nothing relevant is found in your content, the assistant does not invent an
answer. The conversation takes the fallback path you configured, which typically
means a default message or handing the chat to a human agent.

**Can I control the tone the JarCube AI uses?**
Yes. Custom AI instructions let you set the tone it writes in and the topics it
must not attempt. Custom instructions are available on the Business plan and
above.

**Can I stop the JarCube AI from discussing certain topics?**
Yes. You set boundaries in the AI instructions. When a question falls outside what
it should handle, it hands over to a human instead of improvising.

**Can the JarCube AI use what it knows about a customer?**
Yes. The assistant can use CRM fields you already hold about a contact, such as
their plan, last order, or pending request, so replies are specific to the person
asking.

**Can I have more than one AI assistant in JarCube?**
Yes. Multiple advanced AI agents are available on the Business plan and above, so
different bots or departments can answer from different knowledge.

**Does the JarCube AI reply in other languages?**
The assistant replies based on your content and the customer's message, and can
handle multiple languages. Answer quality is best where your uploaded content
covers the language your customers write in.

**How accurate is the JarCube AI?**
Accuracy depends on the content you give it. The assistant only answers when it
finds a confident match in your material, and stays quiet or hands over when it
does not. That design prevents confident wrong answers.

**Does JarCube show which source an AI answer came from?**
Yes. Answers carry the sources they were drawn from, so you can check where a
reply came from and fix the underlying document if it was wrong.

**Can the JarCube AI search my records in plain language?**
Yes. Structured data can be searched in natural language, so a customer or agent
can ask a normal question and the assistant looks up the matching record, and an
automation can be triggered from the result.

**Can I see how much AI usage my account has consumed?**
Yes. The dashboard reports AI usage, including the number of AI answers generated
and the tokens consumed, along with recent activity. This lets you see what the AI
is actually handling.

## 10. Flow builder and automation

**What is the JarCube flow builder?**
The JarCube flow builder is a visual canvas where you build the path a customer
takes through a conversation: what you ask, what you send, and what happens based
on their answer. You drag blocks into place. No code is involved.

**What can a JarCube flow do?**
A flow can ask a question, branch on the answer, send a document, book a slot,
save a field to the contact record, call your system with a webhook, add the
person to a segment, raise a ticket, or hand the chat to an agent.

**What building blocks does the JarCube flow builder have?**
Blocks include start, user input, bot response, AI response, ticket creation, set
attributes, close chat, file attachment, question sets, FAQ, webhook, add to
segment, remove from segment, transfer to agent, go to step, fallback, open
template, and success, failure, and timeout branches.

**Can JarCube flows use buttons instead of typing?**
Yes. Interactive buttons and list pickers let customers choose an option rather
than typing. That means fewer typos, fewer misread answers, and a faster path to
the outcome.

**Does information carry through a JarCube flow?**
Yes. What a customer tells you early in a flow is available later in the same flow
and afterwards in their contact record, so nobody is asked the same question
twice.

**Do I need to redeploy after editing a JarCube flow?**
No. Edit a live flow and the next conversation uses the new version. There is no
release process and no waiting on a development cycle.

**Can JarCube collect files from customers?**
Yes. The file attachment step lets a customer send a document or image inside the
conversation, which is stored against their record for your team to review.

**Can JarCube send documents to customers automatically?**
Yes. A flow can deliver a brochure, price list, floor plan, report, or form the
moment a customer asks for it, without an agent finding and attaching the file.

**Can JarCube book appointments?**
Yes. Appointment booking is available from the Growth plan. Customers pick a slot
inside the chat, get a confirmation, and receive a reminder before the
appointment.

**Can JarCube send automated follow-ups?**
Yes. Automated follow-ups and reminders go out on their own schedule based on what
the customer did, so nobody has to keep a mental list of who needs chasing.

**Can JarCube qualify leads automatically?**
Yes. Lead qualification asks about budget, timeline, or requirement before a
salesperson gets involved, so your team spends time on the enquiries worth
following.

**What is a fallback in a JarCube flow?**
A fallback is what happens when the customer says something the flow did not
expect or the AI cannot answer confidently. You decide the fallback: repeat the
question, send a default message, or transfer to a human agent.

**How many flows can I build in JarCube?**
Starter allows 5 flows, Growth allows 25, and Business and Enterprise allow
unlimited flows.

## 11. CRM, contacts and segments

**What does the JarCube CRM store?**
The JarCube CRM stores each customer as a contact with their name and contact
details, the channel they came from, tags, custom fields collected during
conversations, their full chat history, and any tickets raised for them.

**Can I add custom fields to JarCube contacts?**
Yes. Custom fields let you store whatever matters to your business, such as
vehicle model, course applied for, policy number, or preferred branch. Flows can
fill these fields automatically from what the customer tells you.

**Can I tag contacts in JarCube?**
Yes. Tags let you label contacts by interest, stage, product, or anything else,
and later filter or target them. Tags can be applied manually by an agent or
automatically by a flow.

**What are segments in JarCube?**
Segments are audiences built from contact fields, tags, and past behaviour, such
as customers who ordered last month or leads who never replied. Flows can add or
remove people from a segment automatically, and campaigns target segments.

**Can I see a customer's full history in JarCube?**
Yes. Each contact record shows the complete conversation history across channels,
so an agent picking up a chat can see everything that happened before.

**Can I import my existing contacts into JarCube?**
Yes. Existing contact lists can be imported into the CRM during setup so you are
not starting from zero. We help with this during onboarding.

**Can I export my contacts from JarCube?**
Yes. Your contacts and conversation data remain yours and can be exported. If you
decide not to continue after the free 3 months, we export your data for you.

**Does JarCube track website visitors?**
Yes. Visitors to your site who engage with the chat widget are tracked as records,
so an anonymous visitor becomes a known contact once they share details.

## 13. Tickets and service requests

**Does JarCube support tickets?**
Yes. A conversation can raise a ticket, either automatically from a flow step or
manually by an agent. The ticket keeps the request tracked until it is resolved
rather than relying on someone remembering.

**When would a JarCube ticket be created?**
Tickets suit requests that cannot be resolved in the chat immediately, such as a
refund request, a complaint, a service callout, or an issue that needs another
team. The flow can create the ticket and confirm to the customer that it is
logged.

**Can customers raise service requests through JarCube?**
Yes. Service requests are captured in chat and routed to the right team, with the
customer's details and history already attached.

## 22. Common questions and troubleshooting

**My JarCube bot is not replying. What should I check?**
Check that the bot is published and active, that the flow has a start step, and
that your channel is still connected. If the channel shows disconnected, reconnect
it from the dashboard. If it persists, contact support@jarcube.com.

**The JarCube AI is giving wrong answers. How do I fix it?**
Wrong answers usually mean the source content is outdated or missing. Check the
sources on the answer, correct or remove that document or knowledge entry, and add
the right content. The assistant uses the updated material immediately.

**The JarCube AI says it cannot help. Why?**
The assistant only answers when it finds a confident match in your content. If the
topic is not covered by anything you uploaded, it hands over rather than guessing.
Add that topic to the knowledge base and it will answer next time.

**My uploaded file shows as failed in JarCube. Why?**
A file fails when no text can be extracted from it. Scanned images saved as PDFs
are the usual cause, because there is no text layer to read. Upload a text-based
PDF, DOCX, or TXT version instead.

**My JarCube widget is not showing on my website. What should I check?**
Confirm the script snippet is present on the page and loading without errors, and
that the correct bot ID is set. A browser console error usually shows the cause.
Support can check the configuration with you.

**My JarCube broadcast did not deliver to everyone. Why?**
Delivery failures are reported with a reason per contact. Common causes are an
invalid number, a contact who has blocked your business, or a template that was
not approved. The campaign report lists which and why.

**Can I stop the JarCube bot for one specific conversation?**
Yes. Transferring a chat to a human agent takes the bot out of that conversation
so the agent handles it directly.

**Why did my customer get asked the same question twice in JarCube?**
That usually means the answer was not saved to a field, or two flows both ask it.
Use the set attributes step to store the answer, and it stays available for the
rest of the flow and afterwards in the contact record.
